Refund Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Because store items are digital products, please review your order and Minecraft username carefully before completing payment.

When to contact support

Contact Mushland support if a paid item was not delivered, was delivered to the wrong account because of a store error, or is not working as described.

Eligibility

Refund requests are reviewed individually. Duplicate charges, failed deliveries caused by a technical issue, and unauthorized transactions may be investigated with the payment provider.

Non-refundable situations

We may be unable to refund items that were correctly delivered, used, transferred, or purchased with an incorrectly entered Minecraft username.

How to request help

Send your order details and Minecraft username to Mushland support through Discord. Do not share passwords or payment card numbers.
