Privacy Notice

Effective date: September 17, 2026

This notice explains the information used by the Mushland Store to provide its storefront and digital purchase experience.

Information we use

The storefront may store your Minecraft username and cart contents in your browser. If you contact support or place an order, additional information may be needed to answer your request or fulfill that order.

Minecraft profile lookup

When you enter a Minecraft username, the storefront may request public profile and skin information from Mojang services to display a skin preview. The username is not an authentication credential.

Storage and sharing

Cart, login preference, and consent choices are stored locally in your browser. Payment providers, server operators, or delivery services may process information when those services are connected to the store.

Your choices

You can clear local browser storage at any time. For questions about information connected to an order, contact Mushland support through Discord.
