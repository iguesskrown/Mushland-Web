Cookie Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

The current Mushland storefront does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It uses browser local storage for essential storefront features.

Essential browser storage

Local storage remembers your cart, Minecraft username preference, and cookie-consent choice. This keeps the storefront usable between visits.

Third-party services

The skin preview may request public profile and skin data from Mojang services. External payment or analytics services may have their own policies if they are added later.

Your choices

You can decline optional consent in the storefront banner or clear local storage through your browser settings. Clearing storage may remove your saved cart and preferences.
